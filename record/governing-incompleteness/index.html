Governing Incompleteness: Coordinative Adequacy and the Limits of Computational Observation

UNDER REVIEW · KYBERNETES

This conceptual paper develops coordinative adequacy, consequential authority, reflexive effects and computational restraint to examine when incomplete computational representations become consequential in organizations.

STATUS

Under peer review. This page records the public status; it does not publish the revised manuscript.

CLAIM

Incompleteness becomes a governance problem when task-relevant loss enters authorized decision or action and produces consequences.

BOUNDARY

The paper does not claim that all representations are unusable, nor that incompleteness alone determines organizational failure.
